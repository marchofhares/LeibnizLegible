## Method

- A record's holding is its shelfmark's prefix read against a token table built from the export (the place names the catalogue uses, the abbreviations and words that name an institution, the connectors inside a name); a prefix the table does not know is filed by its place alone, and a shelfmark with no place in front as `(printed witness, no manuscript named)`.
- `(private collection)`: the catalogue writes *Privatbesitz*; `(whereabouts not stated)`: a whereabouts in parentheses, a question mark or *Verbleib unbekannt*; `(no shelfmark)`: an empty shelfmark.
- Digitised copies are the catalogue's `URL_Ms` links counted by host; whether a link opens page images, a finding aid or a catalogue record differs by host and is not checked here.
