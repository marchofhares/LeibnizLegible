# Reach of the audit's patterns across the mint

297,424 open-bucket minted lines on 16,843 pages, from 5,526 catalogue records (5,513 with a Textart). Read-only over the store; per-line flags in `data/gt/flags.jsonl`.

*hyphen*: HTR line ends in a hyphen mark, minted text in a letter (what the re-mint with `keep_hyphen` changes). *math*: the audit's density cut (misses inline algebra in prose). *addition*: a proxy — the line's page has an overlap or short-line fraction at the heavy-revision cut; it marks pages where inline additions were seen, not the lines. *eigh*: the record's Textart says the piece is in its author's own hand — on a letter Leibniz received, the correspondent's. *leibniz*: of those, the records that name no sender (a writing) or Leibniz as the sender (his draft or letter): Leibniz's own hand. Both shares are of the lines whose record has a Textart. *marginalien*: the work is an annotated printed book. *bracket*: an editorial bracket in the minted text. *lh35*: the work's shelfmark is LH 35.
