# The GWLB letter pool, parsed (K2b Task 1)

2,533 letters on 3,579 PDF pages of the checked volumes (1708, 1709, 1710, 1711, 1712, 1713, 1714, 1715, 1716), parsed from the text layer: the running head, the margin line numbers and the notes at the page foot dropped, each letter's number, heading, dateline, *Überlieferung* block and reading text kept apart. Language per letter by the K1 stopword identifier; the siglum is the first witness's (L Leibniz's hand, l his scribe, K the correspondent's hand, k the correspondent's scribe; *other*: a copy, a print, none named); *placeable*: the first witness is in the GWLB's Leibniz collections (LBr, LH, LK, Marg) with a `Bl.` range. The pool is CC BY-NC 4.0 (nc bucket): this report carries counts, no text.
